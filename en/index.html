How Do You Know Which Product Is Better Value?

Supermarket shelf tags and wholesale bundle labels often make it difficult to tell which option actually costs less. Different package sizes and multi-buy promotions mean the lowest sticker price is not always the smartest purchase. With a unit price comparison calculator, every product is normalized into one objective metric: cost per unit.

1. Compare by Weight (Grams & Kilograms)

Ideal for pantry staples like rice, sugar, ground coffee, flour, or snacks. Enter weights using the same unit (for example, all in grams). If Item 1 is 250 g and Item 2 is 1 kg, enter 1000 for the 1 kg pack to compare exact price per gram.

2. Compare by Volume (Milliliters & Liters)

Built for liquids such as milk, cooking oil, liquid detergent, shampoo, and bottled drinks. Enter volume in milliliters (ml) or convert liters (1 liter = 1,000 ml) to uncover the lowest price per ml across bottles and refill pouches.

3. Compare by Unit Count (Pcs / Sheets)

For facial tissues, diapers, multi-pack instant noodles, or coffee pods, enter the piece or sheet count in the size field. The calculator divides total checkout cost by total units so you see the exact price per sheet or pack.

4. Small Pack vs. Family Bulk Pack

Larger packs are often assumed to be cheaper, yet targeted store promotions on medium sizes or refill packs frequently beat bulk pricing per gram. Comparing both prevents overpaying for oversized packaging.

5. Percentage Discount Calculations

When an item is marked down by a percentage (such as 20% off), multiply the original price by the remaining percentage (80%) and enter the sale price into the calculator to benchmark it against regular-priced competitors.

6. Multi-Buy & Free Bonus Promos

For wholesale tiers (such as Buy 3) or bonus deals (such as Buy 2 Get 1 Free), open Add Promo / Multi-Buy. The calculator combines total upfront payment and total quantity received into an effective unit price.

How to Use the Hemat Berapa Calculator

Follow these steps in the store aisle or while comparing online grocery listings:

  1. Select items to compare: Two items (Item 1 and Item 2) are ready by default. Tap ➕ Item #3 (up to 4 items) to evaluate additional package sizes at once.
  2. Choose price mode and enter Price: Keep the toggle on Unit when the shelf price applies to a single pack, or switch to Total when entering a combined bundle price.
  3. Enter net weight, volume, or count: Type the package size in Size (ml/gr) using consistent units. If the second item has the exact same package size as Item 1, tap the 📋 = Item 1 shortcut.
  4. Configure multi-buy or bonus promos (optional): Tap 🏷️ Add Promo / Multi-Buy, then choose a quick preset (Buy 1 Get 1, Buy 2 Get 1, Buy 3, Buy 12) or enter custom numbers in Buy (Pcs) and Free (Pcs).
  5. Review unit prices automatically: Once at least two items are filled in, the lowest unit-cost option is highlighted in green with a 🏆 BEST badge and savings breakdown.
  6. Check upfront budget impact: Review the savings percentage and the Budget Note to verify whether a multi-buy deal fits your immediate checkout budget.

Practical Calculation Examples

Three everyday grocery scenarios showing how unit price math works:

Example 1: Small Pack vs. Bulk Pack (Weight in Grams)

  • Product A (Small Pack): $15 for 250 g → $15 ÷ 250 = $0.06 per gram ($6 per 100 g).
  • Product B (Bulk Pack): $25 for 500 g → $25 ÷ 500 = $0.05 per gram ($5 per 100 g).

Verdict: Product B is better value. Buying two 250 g packs of Product A costs $30 for 500 g. Choosing Product B saves $5 (17%).

Example 2: 20% Off Promo vs. Regular Price

  • Product A (20% Off): Regular price $40 for 400 ml. A 20% discount deducts 20% × $40 = $8, bringing the price to $32 ($32 ÷ 400 = $0.08 per ml).
  • Product B (No Discount): Regular price $35 for the same 400 ml size ($35 ÷ 400 = $0.0875 per ml).

Verdict: Enter 32 for Item 1 and 35 for Item 2. Product A saves $3 (9%) over Product B.

Example 3: Buy 2 Get 1 Free vs. 25% Direct Discount

Assume a 250 ml carton is priced at $12 each:

  • Buy 2 Get 1 Free: You pay for 2 cartons (2 × $12 = $24) and take home 3 cartons (750 ml). The effective cost drops to $8 per carton or $0.032 per ml (an effective 33.33% discount).
  • 25% Direct Discount: Each carton costs $12 − 25% = $9 or $0.036 per ml ($27 total for 3 cartons).

Verdict: Buy 2 Get 1 Free saves $3 across 3 cartons ($0.004 less per ml), as long as all 3 cartons will be consumed before expiration.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is unit price?

Unit price is the cost you pay per single standard measurement of a product—such as cost per 1 gram, per 1 milliliter, or per 1 sheet. It is calculated by dividing total checkout price by total weight, volume, or item count.

How do I calculate price per gram or per milliliter?

Use the formula: Product Price ÷ Net Weight or Volume. For example, a 2,000 ml (2-liter) bottle priced at $36 costs $36 ÷ 2,000 = $0.018 per ml ($18 per liter). Always use the same unit across all compared items.

Is a larger package always cheaper per unit?

No. While bulk sizes often lower packaging costs, retailers frequently discount high-turnover medium sizes or refill pouches, making their price per gram or ml cheaper than the largest bottle on the shelf.

How do I compare a Buy 2 Get 1 Free deal?

Enter the single-item price in Unit mode along with its package size, tap Add Promo / Multi-Buy, and select Buy 2 Get 1. The calculator automatically divides the cost of 2 items across all 3 items received (an effective 33.33% discount).

Is Hemat Berapa free to use and does it store my data?

Hemat Berapa is 100% free with no ads, accounts, or backend database. All numbers you enter are processed locally in your browser memory only and are cleared immediately when you refresh the page or tap Reset.
